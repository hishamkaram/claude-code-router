plus preservation of opt-in function-tool strictness on translated routes.
Credential storage and task lifecycle are unchanged.
The discovery implementation is [PR #111](https://github.com/hishamkaram/claude-code-router/pull/111),
merged as `2c1bc3df1a0a48d902e6ac2d103af8ef0f8ff709`.
