alias during provider-backed launches, follow same-session model changes,
preserve native behavior after switching back, and fail closed on unusable
aliases. Unchecked publication items are not claims of completion.

## Known limits

Family overrides are installed when a launch starts with a CCR model alias.
A launch that starts on native Claude and later selects a CCR alias retains
the existing direct model-selection behavior; it does not retroactively add
family defaults to that already-running process.
